How this works

1 · Assess — paste a listing, let Scout name it and rough out a resale range, then run the fee-and-postage maths for a buy/skip verdict.

2 · Track — add the good ones to your Board and move each one along as you buy, list and sell it.

3 · Learn — when you record what a find actually sold for, the Learning tab shows how close your estimate was — and which categories your instinct is reliable in.

The estimate you set here is the number the system grades later. Base it on real eBay sold listings, not asking prices — the Identify step links you straight to them.
